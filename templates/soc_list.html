A statement of conformity is prepared for a drinking-water or waste-water report when the customer asked for measurement of uncertainty / statement of conformity (sample requisition, confirmed on the customer request form FF-17). Steps: requisition → FF-17 raised and authorised by the QC / Lab Manager → analysis and test report → report approved → records (performed, reviewed, approved) → lab test report with decision rule. The test results come from the report, the specification limits from the standard printed on it and the expanded uncertainty from the MOU register. Decision rule: binary statement, simple acceptance (guard band 0). Forms {{ ctrl_req.doc_no }} (request) and {{ ctrl.doc_no }} (record).
