Decision Rule / Statement of Conformity — User Manual

QC module - forms ETAL-LAB-708-FF-17 (customer request), FF-20 (statement of conformity record), FF-18 / FF-19 (lab test report with decision rule), and the accreditation scope register. Applies to drinking-water and waste-water test reports.

1. Purpose

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (7.1.3, 7.8.6) requires the laboratory to apply a documented decision rule when it states conformity with a specification, and to report the rule. In the software a statement of conformity is prepared for a test report when the customer asked for Measurement of Uncertainty and / or Statement of Conformity under "Additional Requirements of Customer" on the sample requisition form, or on the customer request form (FF-17). The register shows those samples under Awaiting statement with the next step for each (raise FF-17, FF-17 follow-up, awaiting the report, awaiting report approval, prepare statement).

Decision rule (as recorded): Binary Statement for Simple acceptance Rule (Guard band = 0), Specific Risk is < 50 % PFA (Probability of False accept). The result is Pass when the measured value is within the specification limits of the standard, otherwise Fail. The expanded uncertainty (k = 2, 95 %) is reported beside each result.

2. Steps

  1. Customer request form (FF-17) - raised when the request is received, before the analysis (contract review, ISO/IEC 17025 7.1.3): record the customer's request (organisation, parameters, type of request, specification limit / decision rule wished). For a new customer demand the sample has no report yet: use Raise FF-17 on Awaiting statement (or Customer request form); the form is pre-filled from the requisition, and from the report once one exists. The QC / Lab Manager records the follow-up action (approved / denied) and authorises the form. The FF-17 governs what is reported: MOU (uncertainty) and / or statement of conformity; when the requisition reads otherwise the page says so - correct the requisition.
  2. Before records can be created the page checks, and lists what is missing: (1) FF-17 raised; (2) it asks for MOU and / or SOC; (3) approved and authorised by the QC / Lab Manager; (4) the test report prepared; (5) the test report approved (its results are final). Create records stays disabled until all five are done.
  3. New statement (from a report) - choose the sample ID. The software loads the drinking-water or waste-water report: customer, parameters with their results, methods and units, the standard's limit as printed on the report, and the expanded uncertainty in force in the MOU register for the parameter. Only the laboratory's accredited parameters (***) - those in force in its Accreditation scope register - can be ticked: the statement of conformity applies to the accreditation scope. Result 1 is the reported result; enter results 2 and 3 (replicates) and their dates. Correct a limit or uncertainty when needed. Create records makes one record per ticked parameter. Waste water: the outlet results are judged; an inlet-only report (inlet / inlet custom limits) is judged on its inlet results. An accredited parameter for which the standard gives no limit (e.g. Calcium, Sulphate under PEQS for drinking water) is recorded for its expanded uncertainty only when the FF-17 asks for MOU: its results print N.A, its U is printed on the report and the statement says no statement of conformity is made for it. When the FF-17 asks for MOU only (no SOC), the report prints the uncertainty column without the Statement of Conformity block.
  4. Record (FF-20) - each parameter's page shows the three results, mean, standard deviation, the Pass / Fail of each result and the graph (results against the specification lines). Sign as Performed (chemist / assistant manager), then Review (QC / Lab Manager) and Approve (CEO). An approved record is locked; the CEO may unapprove it with a reason. "Sign all / Review all / Approve all" on the register act on every record of the sample.
  5. Legends - Legends on the sample's row edits the legend lines printed on its decision-rule report (section 5).
  6. PDFs - the record PDF (FF-20) per parameter; the lab test report with decision rule (FF-18 waste water / FF-19 drinking water): the sample's normal test report, unchanged, with the column "Uncertainty ±U" after the result (N.A for parameters without a statement), *** on the accredited parameters with a statement, the Statement of Conformity block (Result = Pass / Fail) above the signatures and the FF-18 / FF-19 document control at the foot. It carries the test report's own signatures (Analyzed / Reviewed / Approved by), as every test report does; the statement records carry the Performed / Reviewed / Approved signatures. Drafts print with a NOT APPROVED watermark. When a record's limit was corrected, the limit judged is printed.

3. Where the figures come from

ItemSource
Customer, sample, dates, methodsThe drinking-water / waste-water report with that sample ID (newest).
Result 1The result on the report. A result below the limit of quantification (e.g. "<0.0080") is kept as written and judged at the LOQ (0.008); when every result is below the LOQ the mean prints as the LOQ and the standard deviation as "-". Text results such as "N.D." cannot be judged and are left out.
Specification limitThe limit of the standard printed on the report (SEQS / PEQS; waste water: inland-waters column). Both bounds are read from ranges such as "6 - 9"; "< 1000" gives an upper limit.
Expanded uncertaintyThe approved MOU study of the parameter in the laboratory's MOU register (absolute U; a study of sample kind "Sample" is preferred). When the register has none, type the value and its source.
Accredited parameters (***)The laboratory's Accreditation scope register (section 4): the parameters in force for the matrix on the date the record is made. A report name is matched to an entry by its name or the other names given on the entry ("pH @ 25 °C", "pH***", "Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)"); a hardness is matched only to Hardness, never to Calcium.
Customer requirement (MOU / SOC)The customer request form (FF-17). The sample requisition boxes are shown beside it; when they differ the FF-17 governs and the requisition is corrected.

4. Accreditation scope register

QC › Decision Rule / Conformity › Accreditation scope keeps, per laboratory, the parameters the laboratory is accredited for (PNAC, ISO/IEC 17025:2017) for drinking water and for waste water. It replaces the fixed list the software used until 29-10-2026; that list was entered as the starting content and each entry is marked scope reference: to be entered until the QC / Lab Manager confirms it against the PNAC scope.

  1. Accreditation details - accreditation body, standard, certificate number, scope reference (document / issue / revision), issue date and validity. Printed at the head of the register PDF.
  2. Entry per parameter and matrix - parameter (as the module names it: pH, TDS, Hardness, Chloride ...), other names used on reports (comma separated), method, accredited range, scope reference and the date it is in force from (blank = in force, date not recorded).
  3. Add a parameter when the scope is extended; Edit to complete or correct an entry; Withdraw (with date and reason) when a parameter leaves the scope; Reinstate (with reason) if it returns. Entries are never deleted, and every change (who, when, what) is kept in the change history on the page.
  4. Effect - a statement of conformity can be created, and *** is printed on the lab test report with decision rule, only for parameters in force on the date of the record. A later withdrawal does not change statements already made.
  5. PDF - the register prints as the Accreditation Scope Register (document number set under Form numbers) for audits and management review.

Keep the register identical to the current PNAC scope schedule. After each PNAC assessment or scope revision, update the accreditation details and the entries on the date the revised scope takes effect.

5. Legends of the lab test report with decision rule

The lines under the analytical table of the lab test report with decision rule (FF-18 / FF-19) are controlled in the module, separately from the normal test report.

  1. Starting set - when a sample's decision-rule report is first prepared, its legends are the test report's own note and legend lines, followed by the laboratory's standard legends for the matrix (a standard line the report already gives is not repeated, e.g. a *** line). Until the legends are edited, the report prints this starting set, so a change made to the test report's legends is followed.
  2. Edit per report - Register › Legends on the sample's row: reword any line, move it up or down, remove it (Undo before saving), add new lines, and Save legends. The normal test report is not changed. Reset to the starting set discards the edits. Preview report opens the PDF with the legends as saved.
  3. Lock - once every record of the sample is approved the legends are locked with the report; the CEO unapproves a record to change them. Every save and reset is kept in the change history (who, when, number of lines).
  4. Standard legends - QC › Decision Rule › Standard legends, per laboratory and matrix, kept by the QC / Lab Manager: the default lines new reports start with (e.g. *** accreditation scope, Uncertainty ±U at k = 2, N.A, the decision rule agreed on the FF-17). A report whose legends were already edited keeps its own set.

Long legend lines wrap to the page width and continue on a new page when needed; nothing is cut off.

Corrections to an approved record are made by the administrator through the system's correction procedure (management command correct_soc_spec, applied with the owner's approval) with the audit trail kept: each change (old value → new value, reason, date) is written in the record's correction note, printed on the PDF, and the laboratory re-signs the corrected paper copy.

Records inducted from the laboratory's Excel / Word record carry the mark REC; their signatures print as "Signed on the paper record".
