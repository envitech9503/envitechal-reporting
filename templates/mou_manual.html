About this manual

This guide explains the Measurement of Uncertainty (MOU) function of the Envi Tech AL LIMS, the electronic form of record ETAL-LAB-706-FF-01. A study estimates, for one parameter at one level, how far a reported result may lie from the true value: three operators each measure a reference material ten times (Type A), the known uncertainties of the instrument, glassware, reagents and reference material are added (Type B), and the whole is combined into an expanded uncertainty U at 95 % confidence that is quoted on test reports.

If you are new, read the Getting started topics in order, then Entering a study (step by step). A QC / Lab Manager should also read The source library and Review and approval. Use the search box for a word such as "range", "triangular", "repeat" or "PDF".

Getting started Opening the function

You must be logged in. There are three ways in:

  • From QC. Click QC in the top navigation bar. The Measurement of Uncertainty card offers New Study, Register, Source Library and User Manual.
  • From the QC sidebar. Inside any QC page, open the Measurement of Uncertainty section of the left-hand sidebar: Register (current U), Open Studies, All Studies & Versions, New Study, Uncertainty Sources (library), Deletion Log, this manual and, for administrators, Document Control.
  • Directly by address. The register is at /qc/mou/; this manual is at /qc/mou/manual/.
The function has four working areas: the register (one laboratory: the approved uncertainty of every parameter / level), the study page (replicates, budget, result, signatures), the study editor (where the data are typed, with live calculation) and the source library (the laboratory's instruments, glassware, reagents and reference materials with their certified uncertainties).

Getting started One function, two laboratories

Karachi and Lahore keep separate studies, separate libraries and separate document-control numbers in the same function. The Laboratory selector at the top of the register, the library and the editor chooses the laboratory; the system remembers your last choice and otherwise opens the laboratory you are assigned to (Laboratory Reviewers page) or the office on your e-signature profile.

A study belongs to the laboratory selected when it was created. Reviewing and approving follow the same assignments as the control charts: the laboratory's QC / Lab Manager reviews, the CEO / administrator approves.

Getting started A tour of the study page

  1. Header - parameter, level, sample kind (CRM / working standard / sample), version and status, with Edit (drafts only), PDF form, Repeat study and the master data (sample reference, method, range, equipment, temperature, date, coverage factor).
  2. Repeatability data - one row per operator with the replicates, n, mean and SD, and the result boxes: grand mean, combined relative uc, expanded relative U, expanded U in the unit of the measurand and in percent.
  3. Uncertainty budget - every row of the budget with its range, magnitude, relative factor, distribution (divisor), sensitivity coefficient and relative standard uncertainty. Rows marked [lib] came from the library.
  4. Workbook vs recomputed - only on studies inducted from the laboratory's Excel workbooks: the printed figures beside the recomputed ones and the discrepancy notes (see Inducted studies).
  5. Review and approval - who performed, reviewed and approved, the list of versions, and the action buttons your role allows.

How it is calculated The uncertainty model

The function uses the laboratory's established GUM-based relative budget, exactly as on form ETAL-LAB-706-FF-01:

StepFormulaNotes
Per operatorn, mean, SD = √( Σ(xi - mean)2 / (n - 1) )every replicate counts; a blank cell is "not measured", never zero
Type A - repeatabilityurep = (SDA / x) / √nAfirst operator; x = the study's range
Type A - reproducibilityurepro = (√(SDA2 + SDB2 + SDC2) / x) / √(nA + nB + nC)all operators with data; omitted automatically for a single-operator study
Type B - each sourceu = (a / x) / divisor × cia = magnitude, x = the source's range; divisor 1 for Normal (k=1), 2 for Normal (k=2), √3 Rectangular, √6 Triangular, √2 U-shaped
Combineduc = √Σu2over the included rows
ExpandedU = k · uck = 2 (95 % confidence) unless the study says otherwise
Absolute / percentUabs = U × grand mean; U % = U × 100grand mean = mean of all replicates of all operators
All arithmetic runs on the server in one place (QC/mou_calc.py), unit-tested against the laboratory's own workbooks. The editor only displays what the server returns, so the figures on the screen, the register and the PDF can never disagree.

How it is calculated What "Range (x)" means

The laboratory expresses every component relative to a range, then multiplies the expanded relative uncertainty by the measured mean. For the Type A rows x is the method / calibration working range of the parameter (COD 900 or 1500 mg/L, sulphate 70 mg/L, pH 14, TDS 4000 mg/L, AAS 1 - 20 mg/L and so on) - not the level measured. Each Type B row carries its own x (the flask volume, the wavelength, the balance capacity, the reagent purity ...), taken from the library.

The study cannot be saved when the measured mean exceeds the range: a relative factor above 1 has no physical meaning (two historical AAS sheets had this - see the induction notes). Enter the working range of the method.

How it is calculated Distributions, divisors and the magnitude expression

  • Normal (k=1) - a standard uncertainty taken as is (divisor 1). The laboratory's convention is to halve an expanded certificate value in the magnitude itself, e.g. 0.08/2; the system evaluates the expression and prints both.
  • Normal (k=2) - an expanded certificate value entered whole (divisor 2). Use either this or the /2 expression, never both.
  • Rectangular (divisor √3) - a tolerance with equal probability anywhere inside it: reagent purity, resolution of a reading, temperature drift.
  • Triangular (divisor √6) - a tolerance more likely near the centre: class A volumetric glassware.
  • U-shaped (divisor √2) - rarely needed; a cyclic influence such as a controlled temperature.

The magnitude field accepts a number or a simple expression: 0.1, 0.080/2, (28-27.4)*0.000002*500 (the balance temperature-drift term uDt), 0.5/sqrt(3). Anything else is refused with a message.

Entering a study Entering a study (step by step)

  1. Click New Study. Check the Laboratory.
  2. Master data: parameter, level (e.g. "500 ppm"), unit, sample kind, sample reference (CRM product and lot, or the working-standard ID), method, equipment, Range (x), environmental temperature, study date. Leave k = 2.
  3. Replicates: type each operator's ten values in the cells (Tab moves across). Add the analyst's name and date. Add operator for a fourth or fifth, Add replicate column for more than ten. Mean and SD appear as you type.
  4. Budget: the two Type A rows are already there. Pick each Type B source from the library drop-down and click Add - its unit, range, magnitude, distribution and certificate are copied into the row. Use Custom row for a one-off source. Untick the box at the left of a row to keep it on the form but leave it out of the combined uncertainty.
  5. Watch the result boxes - uc, U, U absolute, U % - and the message strip for anything incomplete.
  6. Save study. The study is a draft: open it and click Submit for review when it is complete.
Every field is copied to the browser as you type (draft protection). A power cut, a closed tab or an expired login does not lose the entry: open the editor again and the draft is offered back.

Entering a study The source library

The library holds, per laboratory, every source of Type B uncertainty with its unit, range, magnitude, distribution, sensitivity coefficient, certificate / calibration reference, calibration date and validity. It is maintained by the laboratory's QC / Lab Manager (and the administrator); chemists pick from it.

  • Add / edit on the library page. Karachi's library was seeded from the 37 inducted workbooks (94 entries: spectrophotometer wavelength / resolution / absorbance at each wavelength, AAS resolution per element line, balance, oven, reactor, water bath, flasks, pipettes, micropipettes, burette, measuring cylinders, reagent purities, CRM certificates, pH buffers). Lahore starts empty and enters its own.
  • Snapshot rule: a study copies the values when a source is added. Changing a library entry (re-calibration, new certificate) affects future studies and the next Repeat of an existing one - never an approved study.
  • Validity is informative: an expired entry is flagged in the drop-down, on the study page and in the library, but it never blocks a study (owner decision 21-10-2026).
  • Deactivate an entry that is no longer in use; studies that used it keep their copy.

The PDF carries the watermark NOT APPROVED until approval and SUPERSEDED afterwards, and prints the three e-signature panels: Performed By (the analysts named on the operators, or the submitter), Reviewed By, Approved By.

Unapprove (administrator, with a reason) puts an approved study back to Reviewed; the register then has no current value for that parameter until a study is approved again - the superseded version is not revived automatically.

Review & approval Repeating a study (new version)

Uncertainty is re-evaluated whenever the laboratory decides - after re-calibration, a new CRM lot, a method change or at the annual review. Nothing in the system expires on its own (owner decision 21-10-2026).

On an approved or superseded study click Repeat study: version n+1 is created as a draft with the same master data and budget, the Type B rows refreshed from the library (rows that took new values are noted), and empty replicate cells. Enter the new replicates, save, submit, review, approve - the earlier version is superseded automatically. Only one open version per parameter / level / sample kind is allowed at a time.

Review & approval Inducted studies (from the Excel workbooks)

The Karachi record (37 workbooks, 21-10-2026) was inducted with every replicate and budget row verbatim. The system recomputes each study from the replicates; the workbook's printed figures are kept beside it on the study page (Workbook vs recomputed) with discrepancy notes. The owner's decision: the recomputed figures are the figures of record once the Lab Manager has reviewed and the CEO has approved each study, so the 36 complete studies were inducted as Submitted for review and the one incomplete pH CRM sheet as a draft.

Codes used in the notes: D1 workbook SD summed only replicates 1 - 8; D2 reproducibility pooled two operators although three had data; D3 combined formula omitted a row; D4 blank distribution; D5 magnitude printed differently from the one used; D6 % row divided by a hand-typed value; D7 absolute U used operator A's mean; D8 mean above the range; D10 no budget; D11 single operator; D12 different ranges on the two Type A rows; D13 a row cubed instead of squared; D14 reproducibility divided by √(sum of means).

A reviewer who disagrees with a recomputed figure returns the study to draft with the reason; the chemist corrects the data and resubmits. The workbook figures stay on the page and the PDF for traceability.

Register The register and the lookup

Current U lists the approved study of each parameter / level / sample kind - the value to quote on test reports (U %, and U absolute in the unit). Open lists drafts and studies awaiting review or approval (the amber strip at the top counts them). All versions shows everything, superseded included. Search by parameter, level, method, sample or equipment.

For the later link to test reports the register is also available as JSON: /qc/mou/api/lookup/?location=Karachi&parameter=COD&level=500 ppm returns the current study (uc, U, U absolute, U %, k, approval date); without parameter it returns every current study of the laboratory.

PDF form The printed form

PDF form on the study page prints record ETAL-LAB-706-FF-01: the control box (document number, issue date, issue and revision numbers of the laboratory - TBA until the administrator enters them on Document Control), the master data, the replicate table with n / mean / SD per operator, the full budget, the four result lines, the record notes (induction notes and remarks), the formula line and the three signature panels. The footer address and telephone follow the laboratory.

Managing Deleting a study

The laboratory's QC / Lab Manager and the CEO / administrator may delete a study of any status, always with a reason (at least five characters). The deletion log keeps who, when, why and a complete copy of the data; administrators see both laboratories, a manager his own.

Managing Document control (administrator)

Each laboratory's document number, issue date, issue and revision numbers for the MOU form are entered once on Document Control and printed on every PDF of that laboratory. Karachi's number ETAL-LAB-706-FF-01 is pre-set; Lahore enters its own.
