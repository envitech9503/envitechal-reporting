Control charts
Each laboratory issues its own controlled-document number for each form (CRM monthly chart, weekly RM chart, intermediate-check chart). The number, issue date, issue and revision numbers entered here are printed in the control box of every PDF of that laboratory and form; until a number is entered the PDF shows TBA. The PDF footer address and telephone follow the laboratory automatically (Karachi head office / Lahore office).

Charts with their own form number
Where one laboratory keeps separate controlled forms for charts of the same type - for example the Lahore environmental sheets ETAL-LAB-603-FF-07 (laboratory temperature), 07A (laboratory humidity) and 07B (visi-cooler temperature) - the chart carries its own number, issue date, issue and revision numbers here. They are printed in the control box of that chart's pages and PDFs instead of the laboratory's form number above. Clear returns the chart to the laboratory's form number.
