About this manual

This guide explains the Control Charts module of the Envi Tech AL LIMS. A control chart tracks a quality-control result against limits calculated from a mean and standard deviation, so the laboratory can see at a glance whether a method and instrument are still performing as expected. The module replaces the Excel workbooks previously used for form ETAL-LAB-604-FF-11 and covers three activities: the monthly CRM exercise, the weekly RM exercise and the intermediate checks of equipment - see The three chart types.

If you are new, read the three Getting started topics in order, then Recording a monthly result. If you need to set up a new chart (any chemist can), go straight to Creating a new control chart (step by step). Use the search box above or press / to find any topic. Topics marked QC Manager or Administrator need those roles; everything else is for every analyst.

Getting started Opening the module

You must be logged in. There are three ways in:

  • From QC. Click QC in the top navigation bar. The Control Charts card offers Record Result, View Charts, Archive and User Manual.
  • From the QC sidebar. Inside any QC page, open the Control Charts section of the left-hand sidebar: CRM Control Charts, Archive / Record List, Add Chart and, for administrators, Laboratory Reviewers.
  • Directly by address. The chart list is at /qc/control-charts/; this manual is at /qc/control_chart_manual/.
The module has three working areas: the chart list (one laboratory, one year), the chart page (one parameter: the graph, the monthly table, the entry form and the sign-off panel) and the archive (every record of every year and both laboratories).

Getting started Choosing your laboratory

Karachi and Lahore each have their own set of charts. The laboratory selector at the top-left of the chart list switches between them.

  • The first time you open the list, it shows the laboratory you are assigned to (see Laboratory reviewers); if you have no assignment, it shows Karachi.
  • After that, the list remembers your last choice for as long as you are logged in. Switching laboratories is one click.
  • Every chart page, PDF and archive row states its laboratory, so a Lahore chart can never be mistaken for a Karachi one.

Getting started A quick tour of a chart page

Click any parameter name in the list to open its chart page. From the top down you will find:

  1. Header — the parameter and level (e.g. Copper 1 ppm), the laboratory, a year selector, and buttons for All charts, PDF form and, when you are allowed to change them, Edit master and Baseline (administrators; also the chemist who created the chart until its first result / first baseline).
  2. Chart details — equipment and ID, method, CRM detail, certified value and range, description, the current baseline version with its mean and standard deviation, and the four limits.
  3. The graph — monthly results plotted as blue diamonds against the green baseline mean, the amber warning limits (±2 SD) and the red action limits (±3 SD). A point that falls outside a limit is filled amber or red.
  4. Monthly table — twelve rows, January to December, with the date, result, status, remark, who performed it, and the baseline result and limits for that row.
  5. Entry form — date, result and remark, with a live preview of the status before you save.
  6. Sign-off panel — Performed by / Reviewed by / Approved by, and the Review and Approve buttons for those entitled to use them.

How control charts work What a control chart is for

Each month the laboratory analyses a CRM of known concentration using the same method and instrument as routine samples. If the method is stable, the result should land close to the value obtained when the baseline was established. Plotting every month's result on the same chart turns that check into a picture: a run of points drifting towards a limit is visible long before a result actually fails.

The procedure is ETAL-LAB-P-604. Two intermediate-check exercises are planned each year (one after calibration, one before proficiency testing); the February exercise, performed before PT, provides the baseline for the year.

Use the Activity selector on the chart list to show one type at a time; the activity pill is shown in the first column. The chart page, the archive and the PDF form adapt their labels (Monthly CRM result, Weekly RM result, Intermediate check reading) and their period (year, or month-year for intermediate checks).

Intermediate checks - a statistical note. Because the limits of a run are computed from the same readings they judge, a single reading can only fall outside ± 2 SD once the run holds about six readings or more (with three readings no point can ever exceed the limits). Statuses of a run are re-evaluated automatically every time a reading is added, edited or deleted, so a reading that was "in control" when entered may become a warning as the run grows - this mirrors the Excel sheet.

How control charts work The baseline and how limits are calculated

A baseline is a set of repeat results for the CRM — usually twelve from the February intermediate check. From them the system calculates:

QuantityHow it is calculatedShown as
Baseline meanAverage of the baseline valuesGreen centre line
Standard deviation (SD)Sample standard deviation of the baseline values (the same as Excel's STDEV)In the chart details
UWL / LWL (warning)Mean ± 2 × SDAmber dashed lines
UL / LL (action)Mean ± 3 × SDRed dashed lines

Baselines are versioned. When an administrator establishes a new baseline (normally once a year), it becomes version 2, 3 and so on; results that were recorded under the earlier version keep the limits they were judged against, so history is never rewritten.

The numbers you see on screen are the same ones the Excel workbooks produced — the seeded baselines were checked against the original sheets value by value.

How control charts work Result status: In control, Warning, Out of control

Every result is classified automatically the moment it is saved:

StatusMeaningWhat you must do
In controlWithin mean ± 2 SDNothing further; a remark is optional.
WarningBeyond ± 2 SD but within ± 3 SDA remark / corrective action is mandatory — the result cannot be saved without one.
Out of controlBeyond ± 3 SDA remark / corrective action is mandatory; investigate per ETAL-LAB-P-604 before continuing routine analysis.

The chart list shows the worst status of the year for each parameter, so a laboratory manager can scan the list and see where attention is needed.

Monthly entry Recording a monthly result

Any logged-in analyst can record a result. Open the chart (QC → Control Charts → choose laboratory → click the parameter) and use the entry form below the table:

  1. Enter the date the CRM was analysed. Today's date is pre-filled.
  2. Type the result in the chart's unit (shown beside the box). As you type, the form previews the status — green, amber or red — against the current limits.
  3. If the preview is amber or red, the remark box becomes mandatory: record what was observed and what corrective action was taken.
  4. Click Record result. The point appears on the graph and the table row fills in, stamped with your name and the time.
One result per calendar month. If a result already exists for that month the save is refused — use the Edit (pencil) button on that row instead. The date cannot be in the future.
Values are accepted with up to the chart's configured number of decimals (usually 2; 3 for pH, fluoride and nitrate; 4 for the AS-13 absorption spectrometer).

Monthly entry Correcting or deleting a result

  • Edit — click the pencil on the table row; the entry form loads that result. Change the date, value or remark and save. The status is recalculated.
  • Delete — only an administrator of that laboratory can delete a result (the bin icon). Deletion is permanent and is recorded in the history log.
  • RM and intermediate-check charts accept one result per date (several in one month); CRM charts accept one result per month.
If the year had already been reviewed or approved, any change to a result clears the review and approval and the year goes back to "Open". This is deliberate: a signed form must match the data. The message on screen tells you when this has happened.

Monthly entry "Results for this year are approved and locked"

Once the CEO / administrator has approved a chart's year, its results are locked: analysts can view but not add, edit or delete. If a late correction is genuinely needed, ask an administrator of that laboratory to Unapprove the year, make the change, and have it reviewed and approved again.

Review & approval The three-step sign-off

Each chart is signed off once per calendar year, mirroring the three signature boxes on the paper form:

Review & approval QC Manager Reviewing a year

  1. Open the chart and choose the year.
  2. Check the table: every month that was analysed has a result, every amber or red row has a meaningful remark.
  3. Click Review in the sign-off panel. The badge changes to "Reviewed by … — awaiting approval".

Undo review is available until the year is approved. You can review only charts of the laboratory you are assigned to; the button is hidden on the other laboratory's charts.

Review & approval Administrator Approving and locking a year

  1. Open the chart and year; confirm the badge shows Reviewed.
  2. Click Approve & lock. The year is locked for analysts and the PDF loses its watermark.

Unapprove reopens the year (confirmation required). An administrator who is assigned to a laboratory can approve only that laboratory's charts; unassigned administrators can approve either.

Review & approval Administrator Laboratory reviewers

QC sidebar → Control Charts → Laboratory Reviewers (admin) is where QC / Lab Managers are assigned to a laboratory. The rules are:

  • A user assigned to a laboratory may review that laboratory's charts only; if the user is also an administrator, they may approve that laboratory's charts only.
  • Administrators with no assignment (CEO / system administrators) review and approve globally.
  • Users with no assignment fall back to the "QC Manager" group or a QC/Manager signature role (global review) — the original behaviour.

To assign: pick the user, pick the laboratory, click Assign. To remove: the bin icon. Current assignments: Hina — Karachi; Sana — Lahore.

Creating & managing charts All analysts Creating a new control chart (step by step)

You need a new chart whenever the laboratory starts monitoring a new parameter, a new concentration level of an existing parameter, or the same parameter on a different instrument. Have these to hand before you begin: the CRM certificate (product and lot numbers, certified value and acceptable range), the method reference, the equipment ID and the February baseline results.

Step 1 — Open the chart master form

Go to QC → Control Charts → Add Chart (sidebar) or the + Add Chart button at the top-right of the chart list. Any signed-in chemist can add a chart for their laboratory; choose the correct Location (Karachi or Lahore) in the form.

Click Save. Because a new chart has no baseline yet, the system takes you straight to the baseline page. The chemist who created the chart may establish its first baseline (v1) and correct the master details until the first monthly result is recorded; after that, changes to the master and new baseline versions are made by an administrator.

Step 3 — Establish the baseline

  1. Paste or type the baseline results in the Baseline values box — one per line or comma-separated, normally the twelve February intermediate-check results.
  2. Set Established on (the date of the exercise) and add a short Note, e.g. "February 2026 intermediate check, before PT".
  3. Click Establish new baseline v1. The mean, SD and the four limits are calculated and shown immediately.

Step 4 — Check the chart page

Open the new chart from the list. Confirm the header details, the limits and the unit are what the certificate and method say. The chart is now ready for the first monthly result.

Worked example. Twelve February results for Alkalinity of 100, 101, 98, 102, 100, 99, 98, 102, 98, 99, 100, 102 mg/l give a mean of 99.92, SD 1.56, warning limits 96.79–103.05 and action limits 95.22–104.61 — exactly what the original Excel sheet showed.
A chart is identified by laboratory + activity + parameter + level + equipment ID. Creating a second chart with the same combination would duplicate records — check the list first.

Creating & managing charts Administrator Establishing next year's baseline

Each February, after the intermediate check, open the chart and click Baseline. Enter the new values exactly as in Step 3 above and click Establish new baseline v2 (the version number increments automatically). From that moment new results are judged against the new limits; the baseline history table at the foot of the page keeps every earlier version, and earlier results keep the version they were recorded against.

Creating & managing charts Administrator Editing or retiring a chart

  • New CRM lot or certificate — click Edit master on the chart page, update CRM Detail, value and range, save. The change appears on all future PDFs; every edit is logged in the history.
  • Instrument replaced — if the equipment ID changes, prefer creating a new chart for the new instrument and retiring the old one, so each chart's history belongs to one instrument.
  • Retiring a chart — untick Active in Edit master. The chart disappears from the current list but all of its records remain in the Archive, marked "inactive".

Creating & managing charts Administrator Bulk set-up from Excel workbooks

The 45 Karachi charts and their Lahore copies were created in bulk from the laboratory's Excel control-chart workbooks using the server command seed_control_charts. This is a system-administrator task, not something done from the browser; ask the system administrator if a large batch of new charts is needed. Day-to-day, use Creating a new control chart.

Lists & archive The chart list

The list shows every active chart of one laboratory for one year: parameter, level, equipment, ID, method, baseline mean and SD, how many of the twelve months are filled, the last result, the worst status of the year and the sign-off state. Use the year selector to look back, and the search box to find a parameter, equipment, ID or method. The PDF icon on each row opens that chart's form directly.

Lists & archive The Archive / Record List

The archive (QC → Control Charts → Archive / Record List) lists one row for every chart-year that holds results or a sign-off, across both laboratories and all years. Filter by laboratory, year, status (in control / warning / out of control / no results) and sign-off state (open / reviewed / approved), or type part of a parameter, equipment, ID or method. Each row shows who reviewed and approved it and when; the folder icon opens the record and the PDF icon prints it. Retired charts remain here, marked "inactive".

To pull all approved Lahore records for an audit: Laboratory = Lahore, Sign-off = Approved (locked), then Print each PDF from the row.

Creating & managing charts Administrator Document-control numbers and the laboratory footer

Karachi and Lahore issue different controlled-document numbers for each form (CRM chart, RM chart, intermediate-check chart). An administrator enters them once on QC → Control Charts → Document Control (admin): document number, issue date, issue and revision numbers for each laboratory and form. The control box of every PDF prints the number of the chart's own laboratory and form; until a number has been entered the PDF shows TBA and the chart page shows a reminder.

The PDF footer also follows the laboratory automatically: Karachi charts carry the head-office address and telephone, Lahore charts the Lahore office address and telephone.

PDF form The printed form

PDF form on the chart page (or the PDF icon in any list) produces a one-page A4 form ETAL-LAB-604-FF-11 for the selected year: the document-control box, the chart header, the twelve-month table colour-coded by status, the graph with its limits, the remarks, the SOP note and the three electronic-signature panels. The file name carries the laboratory, parameter, equipment ID and year, e.g. ControlChart_Lahore_Nitrate_VS-03_2026.pdf.

The document-control number, issue date, issue and revision numbers are maintained per laboratory by an administrator in the document-control register.

PDF form The "NOT APPROVED" watermark and signatures

Until the year has been approved, every PDF carries a diagonal NOT APPROVED watermark so a provisional print can never be mistaken for a controlled record. After approval the watermark disappears and the three signature panels show the signature image, name and date-time of the analyst, the QC Manager and the approver. A user without a signature image on file is shown as "Electronically signed".

Troubleshooting Frequently asked questions

"A remark / corrective action is required before it can be saved."

The result is a warning or out of control. Describe the observation and the corrective action in the remark box, then save.

"A result for this month already exists."

Only one result per calendar month is kept. Edit the existing row with the pencil icon.

I cannot see the Review or Approve button.

Review needs a laboratory assignment (or the QC Manager role) for that chart's laboratory; approval needs administrator rights for that laboratory. Also, Review is disabled until the year has at least one result, and Approve appears only after review.

The chart I need is not in the list.

Check the laboratory selector and the year. If the chart has been retired, find it in the Archive. If it has never been created, add it yourself — see Creating a new control chart.

The year was approved but now shows "Open".

Someone changed a result after approval; the sign-off is cleared automatically so that the signed form always matches the data. Review and approve again.

The limits look different from last year.

A new baseline version has been established. The baseline history on the Baseline page shows every version with its mean and SD.
