About this manual

This guide explains the Internal Audit function of the Envi Tech AL LIMS: the electronic forms of the internal-audit procedure ETAL-QMS-P-808 (Annual Internal Audit Plan ETAL-QMS-808-FF-01, Internal Audit Circular FF-03, Internal Audit Report FF-02 with the witnessing record) and of the non-conforming work procedure ETAL-QMS-P-710 (Non-conformance & Corrective Action Form ETAL-QMS-710-FF-01, Root Cause Analysis Form 710-FF-01A and the NC&CA Log 710-FF-02). One function serves both laboratories; every signature on every form is the e-signature of the user who clicked it.

Read Getting started first. The Management Representative / Lab Manager should then read Plan & circular, Audit report and NC & corrective action; everyone else needs Signing (receipt of circulars, acknowledgement of reports, the department's response to an NC).

Getting started Opening the function

  • From Management. Since 24-10-2026 Internal Audit belongs to the Management Records / Reports module. Click Management in the top bar (or the Management Records tile on the home page); the clause map shows Internal Audits (8.8) and Corrective actions (8.7) with their links.
  • The Documents menu. Every Internal Audit page has a green Documents button under the breadcrumb. It opens one panel with every document type and its form number - Annual Audit Plan (808-FF-01), Circulars & Attendance (808-FF-03), Audit Report (808-FF-02), Witnessing Record (808-FF-02 annex), NC & Corrective Action (710-FF-01), Root Cause Analysis (710-FF-01A), NC&CA Log (710-FF-02) and Sign-off & administration - each with its register and its + New action (new plan, new audit or witnessing circular, new audit, new witnessing, raise NC, start RCA) and the log PDF. The Karachi / Lahore switch at the top of the panel changes the laboratory. Beside the button, quick links lead to the Dashboard, Open NCs, My sign-offs and Raise NC.
  • From the Management sidebar. The Internal Audit (8.8) section lists the same document types with their form numbers: Dashboard, Annual Audit Plan, Circulars & Attendance, Audit Reports, Witnessing Records, NC & Corrective Action, Root Cause Analysis, NC&CA Log, Signing, the ISO 17025 checklist master, the Deletion Log and this manual.
  • Registers. Witnessing Records lists every witnessing record with its audit, circular, analysts and signatures (new witnessing: choose the audit). Root Cause Analysis lists every RCA and, below, the raised NC&CAs that have no RCA yet with Start RCA.
  • Directly by address. The dashboard is at /qc/ia/ (short link /management/ia/).
The Dashboard shows, for one laboratory, the audits on record, the open and overdue NC&CAs, the records awaiting your signature, the annual plan of the year, the findings per year and the non-conformances by ISO 17025 section.

Getting started Two laboratories and the roles

Karachi and Lahore keep separate plans, circulars, audits, NC&CAs and document-control numbers. The Laboratory selector at the top of each page chooses the laboratory; the system remembers your last choice.

Getting started The audit cycle in the system

  1. Annual plan (FF-01) - the MR ticks the months in which each department is audited; the CEO approves.
  2. Circular (FF-03) - the MR issues the notice with the schedule; every person listed signs receipt. A separate witnessing circular announces the tests to be witnessed.
  3. Audit report (FF-02) - started for one standard: ISO/IEC 17025:2017, ISO 9001:2015 or ISO 14001:2015. The checklist comes from that standard's master list (or from the previous audit of the same standard). The auditor records the findings and the result of every clause, the summary, the time frame and the conclusion, and signs as auditor; the auditee acknowledges; the CEO / Top Management then endorses the report ("I have reviewed and acknowledged the results and findings of this internal audit report"), as on form issue 02 rev 01. The Audit No. / Year (e.g. 01/2025) can be corrected while the report is a draft.
  4. Witnessing record - tests witnessed with assigned and obtained values; signed by the auditor and the analysts.
  5. NC & CA (710-FF-01) - raised from a finding (or on its own for complaints, PNAC assessments, testing); the department records the root cause and corrective action; the MR follows up and closes. Every NC&CA is a row of the log (710-FF-02).
  6. Root cause analysis (710-FF-01A) - causes ticked, the Why chain, conclusion; performed by the MR, reviewed by the CEO.

Plan & circular Annual Internal Audit Plan (FF-01)

Internal Audit › Annual Audit Plan › New plan. Enter the year and, per department row, tick Due under the month(s) the audit is planned. Save, then Sign as MR; the CEO opens the plan and clicks Approve as CEO, which locks it.

As the audits take place the MR ticks Done (the done? button on the plan page, allowed even after approval, because "Done" records execution and does not change the plan). The PDF prints the paper form with the planned months in amber and the completed months in green, and the two e-signatures.

One plan per laboratory and year. Editing a plan that the MR has signed clears that signature; the CEO can unapprove with a reason.

Plan & circular Circular and attendance (FF-03)

Circulars › Audit circular (or Witnessing circular). The notice carries To / From, the audit number and date, the schedule rows (day, time, scope, criteria, auditors, auditee) and the attendance list, pre-filled from the last circular of the same kind. Link the circular to its audit report once the report exists (or create the circular from the report page).

Issue as MR locks the circular and signs it. Every person listed then signs receipt on the circular page or on the Signing page; a staff member who is not listed can still sign and is added to the list. The PDF prints each receipt e-signature with its date.

Withdraw (MR, with a reason) returns an issued circular to draft and clears the receipt signatures.

Audit report Starting an audit

Audit Reports › New audit. Choose the year and whether the checklist comes from the previous audit (default: the clause wording, the laboratory's QMS references and the last findings are carried forward with the results cleared, so the auditor revises rather than retypes) or from the master list (blank). The audit number is assigned as nn/yyyy per laboratory and year.

The report opens in the editor: header (dates, auditor, auditee, address, scope), the checklist, then the time frame, conclusion and remarks.

Audit report Working through the checklist

Each row is one clause of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (or a PNAC policy): the requirement as the laboratory's checklist words it, the lab's reference to its QMS, the auditor findings and the result - Conforms, Observation, Minor NC, Major NC or Not applicable; heading rows are left blank. A tag (NC-01, MNC-02, Obs-01) labels the finding the way the reports do.

  • Use the filter box to jump to a clause or a word, and show rows with a finding only to review the NCs.
  • + inserts a row after the current one (a sub-clause not in the master, or a continuation row); the remove tick drops a row.
  • The summary (observations, minor, major) is counted from the Result column when you save; the counter in the title shows it while you work.
  • Your typing is kept as a draft in this browser until the save succeeds, as on the control-chart forms.

On the report page Findings only lists the observations and NCs; Raise NC beside a finding opens the NC&CA form pre-filled with the clause and the finding text.

Audit report Signing the report

When the checklist is complete the auditor clicks Sign as internal auditor (the dates and the auditee must be entered). The report is locked and awaits the auditee, who clicks Sign as auditee on the report page or on the Signing page. The PDF prints both names and e-signatures with the signature dates (the printed date can be set in the editor; blank = the date of signing).

Reopen (MR, with a reason) returns a signed report to draft and clears both signatures; the reason is kept in the remarks.

Audit report Witnessing record

On the report page click Add witnessing: date, the auditor, the witnessing circular, the analysts witnessed (each signs in the system) and one row per test (parameter, performed by, range, assigned value of the blind sample, obtained value, remarks). The auditor signs first; then each analyst named signs. The record prints after the report in the same PDF with its own signature panels.

NC & corrective action The NC&CA form (710-FF-01)

The form has three sections, filled by three people, as on paper:

  1. Non-conformance - the MR / internal auditor: department, number, date, raised by, standard & clause, raised due to (audit internal / external, testing, calibration, customer complaint, others), the category, the significance evaluation, the nature (priority, risk type, severity, impact on past results), the details and the proposed completion date. Raise & sign as MR signs the "MR / Designee" line and sends the NC to the department.
  2. Concern department section - any member of the laboratory: root cause, corrective action, completion date; Sign department response signs the "Concerned Head of Department" line.
  3. MR section - the MR: follow-up & close-out with, satisfactory Yes / No, dates, description; Follow-up & close signs "NC&CA closed by". Not satisfactory keeps the NC open and records the next follow-up date.

The number is suggested as NC-nn/source/yyy in the laboratory's own style (for example NC-01/IA/026) and can be changed. The log columns (assigned to, target date) are on the same form.

NC & corrective action Root cause analysis (710-FF-01A)

On the NC&CA page click Root cause analysis: tick the main causes and sub-causes (Personnel, Method / Procedure, Environmental Conditions, Management System, QSP, Material, Equipment, Other), state whether the 6-Why technique is required, write the statement of problem (pre-filled from the NC details), the Why chain and the conclusion. Sign as performer (MR) and Review as CEO add the two e-signatures; the PDF reproduces the paper form.

NC & corrective action The NC&CA log (710-FF-02)

NC & Corrective Action is the log: every NC&CA of the laboratory with its serial number, clause, category, reported by, dates, target, follow-up, closing and closed by. Filter by stage (open / closed), source (internal audit, PNAC assessment, customer complaint, testing, calibration, other) or year; overdue targets are shown in red. Log PDF prints the paper log (optionally for one year) with the closing e-signatures.

Rows marked log are entries of the laboratory's historic log for which no NC&CA form exists in the record (the 2024 PNAC assessment NCs, complaints); they are kept for the register and can be completed later. Rows marked REC were inducted from the laboratory's forms.

Signing The Signing page

Internal Audit › Signing lists everything you can sign now in the selected laboratory - circular receipts, reports awaiting your acknowledgement, witnessing records, NC&CAs awaiting your department response, and, for the MR / CEO, plans to sign or approve, circulars to issue, reports to sign, NC&CAs to raise or close, RCAs to perform or review. Tick the records and click Sign selected; each goes through the same checks as the button on its own page and anything that does not pass is listed with the reason. The dashboard tile My sign-offs counts them.

PDF forms The PDF forms

Every record prints as its paper form with the Envi Tech AL header, the document-control box (number, issue date, issue and revision numbers - per laboratory, maintained by the administrator on Document Control) and the laboratory's footer: the Annual Plan (landscape grid, amber planned / green completed), the Circular (schedule and attendance with receipt e-signatures), the Audit Report (clause table over as many pages as needed, summary, time frame, conclusion, auditor / auditee panels, then the witnessing record), the NC&CA form (the tick-box form with the three signature lines), the RCA (landscape with the cause grid and the Why boxes) and the Log. A record that is not fully signed carries a watermark (DRAFT, AWAITING AUDITEE, OPEN ...).

Managing The master checklist

Checklist masters (ISO 17025 / 9001 / 14001) hold one clause list per standard (ISO/IEC 17025:2017 4.1 … 8.9.3 and the PNAC policies; ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 clauses 4 … 10.3, to which the laboratory adds its own checklist wording) with the laboratory's checklist wording. QC / Lab Managers and the administrator can add a clause, edit the wording or mark a clause inactive. A new audit started from the master uses the active clauses; audits started from the previous audit keep that audit's rows. Findings always store their own copy of the wording, so editing the master never changes a signed report.

Managing Document control, deletion and history

  • Document Control (administrator): number, issue date, issue and revision of each of the six forms, per laboratory.
  • Delete (MR / CEO, with a reason): a copy of the record (findings, witnessing, RCA) is kept in the Deletion Log. A report with NC&CAs linked cannot be deleted until they are deleted or unlinked.
  • Every change is kept in the record history (simple_history), and every reopen / unapprove / withdraw stores who, when and why.
  • Records inducted from the laboratory's Word / Excel files carry the REC mark and Record notes describing anything found inconsistent in the original (numbering, dates, clause references).
