Intermediate checks User Manual
Each piece of equipment has one monthly chart per check standard (e.g. weight 1 g, 10 g, 100 g). Its baseline is the equipment's intermediate-check run (procedure ETAL-LAB-P-604), so no new baseline study is needed: the monthly results are plotted against the mean ± 2 SD (warning) and ± 3 SD (action) of that run. When the next intermediate check is carried out, it can be adopted as a new baseline version on the chart's Baselines page.
