How revisions work. Each form keeps its full revision history. Issue new revision (administrator) when the laboratory revises a form: enter the new document number, issue date, issue and revision numbers, the effective date and the reason - documents dated on or after the effective date print the new revision, every document dated earlier keeps the revision it was issued with (report, plan, circular, audit, NC, RCA, study, preparation or job date; a control-chart sheet follows the end of its month or cycle, so the sheet still open takes the new revision and closed periods keep theirs). Correct only fixes a typing error in an existing revision; the previous values stay in the audit history. Revisions are never deleted. Analysis reports (drinking water, waste water, ambient air, gaseous, noise, lux, ...) store their form number on each report and are not affected by this register.
